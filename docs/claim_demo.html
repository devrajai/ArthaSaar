A channel says "Nifty 26,000 soon". That is a claim with a number in it, which makes it falsifiable. This turns the target into the move it requires, then asks a simpler question: over ten years, how often did the Nifty move that far within that many sessions?

It does not say the claim is wrong. A 60th-percentile move is entirely possible — it is just not insight. The value is in replacing "soon" with a number.

What this does not do

No claim is scored as right or wrong — only the size of the move it requires is measured against history. Only a target level written next to an index name, with a forward-looking word beside it, is parsed; "Nifty fell to 22,500" is a description, not a claim, and is skipped rather than guessed at.

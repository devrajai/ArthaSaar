For education and research. Not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell. Prices and fundamentals can be wrong, delayed, or missing — check anything you intend to act on against the exchange or your broker.

Where the data comes from. NSE end-of-day bhavcopy and reference files (prices, open interest, indices, surveillance lists, ban list, corporate actions); Yahoo Finance (India VIX, global indices, crypto); AMFI (mutual fund NAVs); publisher RSS feeds for news. Each section names its own source.

Something missing or wrong? Tell me what to add or fix — a one-minute form. It goes to me, not to a third party.

Licence. NSE and BSE data is governed by their Data Sharing & Usage Policy. Personal, non-commercial use is generally fine; redistributing exchange data publicly, or building a product on it, may need a licence from the exchange. This site is a personal project, not a licensed data vendor.
